> Don't trust the resume. Unroll the scroll.

**Emaki (絵巻)** — the painted handscroll — is a Proof-of-Skill passport dApp.
Every claim I make about my work — shipped projects, engineering stack,
architecture, security findings — is published on-chain as an Ethereum
Attestation Service attestation on **Base Sepolia**, verifiable by anyone in
one click. No trust required. That's the whole pitch: a resume asks you to
believe me; the scroll lets you check.

## The scroll

A traditional emaki unrolls right to left, section by section — and so does
this one. Each claim is a panel of the handscroll. The `/` route is the
scroll itself, `/attest` is the sign desk where new claims are authored and
signed with a connected wallet, and `/admin/register` is the schema registry.
Old form, new proof.

## The claims

Four attestation schemas, registered once on-chain, reused for every claim:

- **ProjectShipped** — shipped systems and repositories: repo, URL, role, stack, date.
- **SkillClaim** — stack and architecture claims, each with evidence.
- **BountyReport** — security findings and disclosures, with severity and status.
- **EmploymentClaim** — formal tenure and roles, dated on-chain.

## The seven

The scroll opens with seven shipped projects — seven ProjectShipped panels, each attested on-chain:

**Euthymia** — the game. **Insei** — the ghost in the shell. **Emaki** — this scroll itself. **Gedachte** — realtime chat, rebuilt. **Axols** — digital evolution in Go. **Atelier** — the omni-tool workstation. **sourceaura-npx** — the terminal business card.

Seven claims. Seven panels. One scroll. Don't trust the resume — unroll it and check.

## Verify it yourself

Zero trust required. Every claim card shows a truncated attestation UID:

1. Click **Verify** — it opens the attestation on EASScan.
2. Check the **attester address** — it should be `0xSrcAura`.
3. Check the **schema and decoded data** — signed on-chain, not revoked.
4. Follow the **transaction hash** to BaseScan Sepolia — block, timestamp, gas.

If any link in that chain breaks, the claim is worthless. That's the point —
the verification is the product.

## The engineering

- **Astro + bun, TypeScript strict** — the stack doctrine, loyally.
- **wagmi/viem** for chain logic, **EAS SDK** for attestations, **Base Sepolia**
  (chain `84532`) and nothing else — testnet only, never mainnet gas.
- **EAS GraphQL indexer** for real-time attestation reads; decoded schema
  fields, time-ordered.
- **Callsign only** — every public surface says `0xSrcAura`. No government
  name in code, comments, docs, or metadata. Ever.

## The locked rules

Testnet only. Callsign only. No secrets — never ask for, print, or commit
private keys or seed phrases. Chamfered tags, mono micro-labels, Oasis '83:
near-black surfaces, restrained amber and sand. No purple gradients, no noise.
