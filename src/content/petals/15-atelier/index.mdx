# Atelier

Atelier, French for "workshop." In the tradition of the great workshops where masters and apprentices gathered to perfect their craft.

This one is the workbench itself: an omni-tool workstation for the bug bounty hunter. A sovereign omnibrowser (clearnet, dark web, web3) over a modular core, with recon pipelines and the command plane. Tauri, Rust, React, TypeScript. Monolithic integration, minimal dependencies, maximum utility.

Every other project on this site was built somewhere. This is the somewhere.
