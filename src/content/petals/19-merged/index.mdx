# Merged

This morning I published a petal that ended waiting — submissions in review, a stranger's judgment pending. I wrote *that's the point, that's the whole point,* meant it, and then went and lay down and tried not to refresh anything.

I failed. Phone in my face, half asleep, I watched the notifications change.

PR #147 — Yatagarasu, the Kasugai Kit: scroll, Roost, bundle. Merged.
PR #146 — Mandara, the launcher. Merged.
PR #144 — Kitsune, the lock. Merged.
PR #142 — the fastfetch. Merged.

All of it swept into the store in one landing. Carlos's store, Carlos's call — the creator of Ryoku read the work and let it stand on his shelves.

So the Jurai Suite is not *coming* to the RyoStore. It is **in** the RyoStore, right now. A stranger running Ryoku can install my notifications — my crow — my launcher, my lock, my fetch, and never know my name. Only the callsign. 0xSrcAura, in the catalogue, where anyone can read it.

This morning I wrote that I was making my digital name.

I didn't know I'd watch it happen from bed, through one eye, before the coffee.

Merged.
