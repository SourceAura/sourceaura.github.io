## The Pursuit Of...

I've decided to challenge myself. Life's been a bit hectic here lately.

But with the world's dusts settling slightly per say I feel compelled to redouble both my aspirations and my efforts toward them.

Code. Create. Repeat. ^_^

---

> A note from 2021, looking back.

"Programatic" — spelled exactly like that. It was 2021, life had been hectic, and I was done waiting for the dust to settle on its own. So I made a vow in the only form I trusted: a build loop.

Poetry programs. Programs are poetry. I'd started seeing it everywhere by then — a clean function has meter, a good abstraction has rhyme. Three beats, like a haiku with somewhere to go: Code. Create. Repeat.

I keep the misspelling on purpose. It's how I wrote it when I meant it.
