# Re-Vamp(ire) 🤯

Re-vamp. It's a pun and it's not.

A vampire, sure: that's Gengar, one of my favorites. Purple ghost, grinning out of the dark. But also *revamp*: to redo, to rebuild. A moment of remembrance: like Dracula, like Alucard, remembering who the fuck they are.

I'd been drifting. Then something old in me sat up and said get back on your shit. So here we are. Really.
